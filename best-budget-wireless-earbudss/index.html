The Anker Soundcore Life P3 gives you active noise cancellation, wireless charging, and a fun, customizable sound profile for a fraction of what competitors charge. The Beats Fit Pro lock securely into your ears with flexible wingtips, so they won’t budge during runs, lifts, or yoga. Their bass-forward sound pumps up your energy, and the ANC blocks gym noise. Our verdict

For most people, the Jabra Elite 7 Pro is the top pick: it balances excellent call quality, good ANC, and all-day comfort at a reasonable price. If you’re on a tight budget, the Anker Soundcore Life P3 delivers nearly everything you need for less money. And if you live in your workout gear, the Beats Fit Pro are the most secure fit you can buy. Whichever you choose, you’re getting impressive performance without overspending.
