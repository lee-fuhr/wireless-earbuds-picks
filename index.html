How we choose our picks

We don’t buy every pair of earbuds on the market ourselves, but we do something arguably more useful: we track long-term owner feedback across forums and retailer reviews, cross-reference those against published expert evaluations from trusted sources, and pay attention to what actually breaks or degrades over months of real use. Poor battery longevity, connectivity dropouts, and uncomfortable ear tips are the most common complaints we look for, if a model shows persistent issues in those areas, it doesn’t make our list. Our picks are based on a combination of reputation, proven reliability, and consistent praise from both reviewers and everyday users. We prioritize earbuds that have been on the market long enough to gather meaningful feedback, and we favour models that offer good value at their respective price points. This approach gives you a shortlist of options that are far more likely to satisfy you after the honeymoon period ends.
